# DOOH Corporate Website Research & Strategy

## Executive Summary
This document outlines the strategic pivot from a self-service booking platform to a world-class international corporate website. The goal is to position the brand as Africa's next iconic Digital Out-of-Home (DOOH) company, ready to partner with multinational brands, investors, and agencies.

## Competitive Research Synthesis

### JCDecaux (Global Leader)
*   **Brand Positioning**: Positions itself as the undisputed global leader. Focuses on unblockable mass media, brand safety, 100% video completion, and the flexibility of digital media combined with data-rich programmatic targetability.
*   **Website Structure**: Information architecture flows from broad industry trends (e.g., reaching connected consumers in the "Active Space") to specific, highly technical programmatic solutions.
*   **Messaging**: Uses authoritative, enterprise-grade language ("Unblockable always-on mass medium", "Turnkey campaign activation").
*   **Visuals**: High-end photography of real-world premium installations, elegant typography, and extensive whitespace.

### Clear Channel Outdoor (Global Leader)
*   **Brand Positioning**: Focuses on "Continuing a legacy of building brands." Emphasizes data-driven results (e.g., their RADAR suite) and measurable campaign impact.
*   **Website Structure**: Provides clear entry points for both novices ("New to OOH") and seasoned programmatic buyers.
*   **Messaging**: Highlights the contrast between "digital burnout" online and the real-world, unskippable impact of physical DOOH.
*   **Visuals**: Professional, data-oriented, and approachable corporate style.

### Ramani.io (African Tech Benchmark)
*   **Brand Positioning**: A high-growth African tech company that feels globally competitive ("Pioneering AI in supply-chain financing").
*   **Website Structure**: Clear value propositions, modern tech aesthetic, partner logos, and straightforward messaging.
*   **Visuals**: Bright, modern, welcoming, not dark or gloomy. Effective use of trust-building elements for both local and international contexts.

## Strategic Direction for (Company Name)

### Brand Identity
*   **Positioning**: Africa's most premium, reliable, and globally competitive DOOH network. We provide world-class digital out-of-home infrastructure to connect global brands with high-value audiences.
*   **Tone of Voice**: Authoritative, visionary, trustworthy, enterprise-ready, and premium.

### Design System & Color Palette
*   **Typography**: Inter (System Sans-Serif). Clean, readable, and highly professional.
    *   H1 Display: Large, bold, tight tracking.
    *   H2 Section: Semi-Bold.
    *   Body: Medium/Regular.
*   **Color System**: Bright, modern, and welcoming (strictly avoiding predominantly black/dark themes).
    *   **Primary Corporate**: Deep Trust Blue (`#0F172A` or `#1E3A8A`) - communicates authority and stability.
    *   **Accent/Innovation**: Vibrant Azure (`#0EA5E9` or `#2563EB`) - communicates technology, movement, and digital focus.
    *   **Backgrounds**: Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) and Soft Grays (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`) - ensures a clean, luxurious feel.
*   **Visuals**:
    *   High-quality, large-scale photography of screens in premium urban locations.
    *   Minimalist, light Lucide icons.
    *   Generous whitespace following a strict Base-8 grid.
    *   Smooth, premium screen transitions using Framer Motion spring physics.

### Information Architecture (Site Map)
1.  **Home (`/`)**: High-impact hero, social proof, fleet highlights, and primary CTAs.
2.  **About Us (`/about`)**: Mission, vision, leadership, and global ambition storytelling.
3.  **Advertising Products (`/products`)**: High-end showcases of digital billboards, street furniture, etc.
4.  **Fleet & Screen Network (`/network`)**: Data-driven page displaying coverage, reach, and technical specs.
5.  **Solutions & Industries (`/services`, `/industries`)**: Use-cases demonstrating DOOH ROI for specific sectors.
6.  **Pricing & Packages (`/pricing`)**: Clear, professional tiers (SME, Agency, Enterprise) emphasizing value without looking cheap.
7.  **Process & Why Us (`/process`, `/why-us`)**: Step-by-step campaign execution and unique value propositions.
8.  **Social Proof (`/case-studies`, `/clients`)**: Impactful metrics, campaign numbers, and partner logos.
9.  **Resources & Legal (`/faq`, `/blog`, `/careers`, `/contact`, `/privacy`, `/terms`)**: Complete informational and corporate architecture.

### Core Philosophy
*   **Trust-First**: No cheap visuals. Every element must communicate billion-dollar scale and enterprise readiness.
*   **Performance**: Instant UI interactions, accessible design, and fast loading times.
*   **Authenticity**: Realistic, professional copy. Absolutely no "Lorem Ipsum" or "Coming Soon" placeholders.
