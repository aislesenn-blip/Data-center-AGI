# AdTech Corporate Website Research & Strategy

## Executive Summary
This document outlines the strategic pivot to a world-class international corporate website. The goal is to position the brand as an innovative AdTech company and mobility advertising platform, specifically focused on connected auto-rickshaw (Bajaj/TukTuk) screens, without exaggerating into roadside billboard ownership.

## Competitive Research Synthesis

### JCDecaux & Clear Channel (Global OOH Leaders)
*   **Brand Positioning**: Enterprise-ready, data-driven, accountable. Focuses on brand safety, programmatic access, and unblockable reach.
*   **Takeaway for Us**: We must use similar enterprise-grade language ("programmatic", "attribution", "dynamic creative", "audience intelligence"), but apply it to our specific mobility/auto-rickshaw context. We are the enterprise-grade solution for a highly localized format.

### Ramani.io (African Tech Benchmark)
*   **Brand Positioning**: High-growth African tech company ("Pioneering AI in supply-chain financing"). Bright, modern, trustworthy.
*   **Website Structure**: Use of bento grids, overlapping cards, vertical segmented controls, and deep integration of social proof (top-tier engineering talent, global corporate alumni).
*   **Takeaway for Us**: Our layout and component architecture should mirror this high-density, interactive, and polished "Speed of Thought" UX. Emphasize talent density and technological edge.

## Strategic Direction for [Company Name]

### Brand Identity
*   **Positioning**: An innovative AdTech company building the future of urban advertising through connected auto-rickshaw screens. A technology-driven mobility advertising platform starting with transit media and expanding over time.
*   **Tone of Voice**: Authoritative, visionary, trustworthy, enterprise-ready, and premium. Transparent about our current network (transit/auto-rickshaws) while demonstrating massive scale and data capabilities.

### Design System & Color Palette
*   **Typography**: Inter (System Sans-Serif). Clean, readable, and highly professional. Strict fluid typography for mobile.
*   **Color System**: Bright, modern, and welcoming.
    *   **Primary Corporate**: Deep Trust Blue (`#0F172A` or `#1E3A8A`)
    *   **Accent/Innovation**: Vibrant Azure (`#0EA5E9` or `#2563EB`)
    *   **Brand Highlight**: Vibrant Green (`#00C800` or similar for specific CTAs/Typewriter effects, if adopting Ramani-style tech cues, but sticking to Azure for our established brand).

### Component Architecture (Ramani-Inspired Depth)
1.  **Typewriter Hero**: Dynamic text cycling through our core value propositions (e.g., "mobility networks", "programmatic transit", "hyper-local reach").
2.  **Bento Grids**: Complex, overlapping cards to break the grid and provide depth, especially for demonstrating the tech stack and social proof.
3.  **Segmented Controls**: Vertical tabs for exploring complex product features (e.g., "For Brands", "For Agencies", "For Fleet Owners") without overwhelming the user or causing layout shifts.

### Core Philosophy
*   **Truthfulness**: Do not claim billboard ownership. We are a mobility AdTech platform.
*   **Mobile Excellence**: The mobile experience must feel bespoke, not just responsive. Meticulous attention to touch targets (48px+), typography scaling, and avoiding cramped spacing.
*   **Depth & Density**: Pages should be comprehensive. No minimal empty spaces; every section should validate the tech, the team, or the reach.
