# Extensive DOOH Corporate Research

## 1. Brand Positioning & Identity
Top-tier DOOH companies like JCDecaux, Ocean Outdoor, Clear Channel, and Broadsign do not position themselves merely as "ad space providers." They position themselves as:
- **Global Tech Innovators**: AdTech pioneers bridging the physical and digital world.
- **Architects of the Urban Experience**: Curators of "The Art of Outdoor" (Ocean Outdoor). They emphasize enhancing the city landscape.
- **Data-Driven & Measurable**: Emphasizing ROI, programmatic capabilities (pDOOH), neuroscience research, and audience measurement (Broadsign, JCDecaux).
- **Sustainable & Responsible**: Focusing on ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance), sustainable street furniture, and eco-friendly operations.

### How they build trust & authority
- Presenting as heavily data-backed (e.g., neuroscience studies showing DOOH is 2.5x to 8x more impactful).
- Providing clear, impressive global statistics (e.g., JCDecaux: 79 countries, 3,895 cities, 1,105,906 panels).
- Holding certifications like SOC 2 (Broadsign) to appeal to enterprise/government clients.

## 2. Website Structure & UX
**Pages typically include:**
- **Home:** High-impact, full-screen video/images of iconic billboards in major cities. Immediate value proposition.
- **Solutions / For Brands:** Focused on the advertiser's journey (Programmatic, Data-driven OOH, Dynamic Messaging).
- **Where We Are / Coverage:** Interactive maps or impressive lists of markets (airports, streets, retail, transit).
- **Insights & Research:** A hub for neuroscience studies, case studies, and ROI proof.
- **Investors:** Highlighting stock information, governance, annual reports, signaling billion-dollar scale.
- **Sustainability / ESG:** Dedicated sections for climate strategy and responsible employer metrics.

**Information Flow:**
1. Visual Impact (The "Wow" factor - iconic locations).
2. Data & Tech (Why it works - programmatic, tracking).
3. Scale & Reach (Where it works - global/local footprint).
4. Social Proof & Case Studies (Who it worked for).

---

## 3. Visual Identity
- **Typography:** Clean, sans-serif, authoritative fonts (e.g., Helvetica Neue, Inter, Circular). Heavy use of bold headlines with generous line height for readability.
- **Colors:** Timeless and premium. Often monochromatic (black, white, grays) accented by one striking color (e.g., deep blue, electric cyan, or vibrant orange) to denote technology and energy.
- **Whitespace:** Extremely generous. Content breathes.
- **Photography & Video:** Massive, high-resolution imagery of their screens in context (busy city centers at night, high-end airports). The screens in the photos are always displaying premium brands (Apple, Nike, High Fashion).
- **Iconography:** Minimalist, thin-line icons.

---

## 4. Messaging
- **Tone:** Confident, visionary, professional, and data-centric.
- **Keywords:** "Omnichannel," "Programmatic," "Data-driven," "Full-motion," "Neuroscience," "Scale," "Impact."
- **Talking to Enterprise:** Focuses on "Brand Lift," "Foot-traffic studies," "Cross-media campaigns," and "Measurable ROI."
- **Selling the Dream:** "Advertising on an Ocean site doesn't just create impact for brands, it elevates a brand from advertising to a piece of commercial art."

---

## 5. Services & Advertising Packages
Premium DOOH companies *rarely* list flat public pricing like a SaaS product.
- **Structure:** Packages are usually based on Environments (Airports, Malls, Transit, Billboards) or Objectives (Brand Awareness, Drive to Store).
- **Pricing Model:** CPM (Cost Per Mille/Thousand impressions), Share of Time (e.g., 10 seconds per minute), or Programmatic RTB (Real-Time Bidding).
- **Sales Funnel:** "Request a Proposal," "Talk to a Media Specialist," or "Book a Discovery Call." They require the client to engage with a salesperson for custom enterprise pricing.
- **Flexibility:** They heavily market flexible buying (daily, day-parting, audience-trigger based) rather than strict monthly contracts.

---

## 6. Social Proof
- **Case Studies:** Deep dives into campaign objectives, strategy, and massive results (e.g., "drove 400,000 store visits").
- **Client Logos:** Only top-tier, recognizable global brands.
- **Scale Metrics:** Billions of impressions, thousands of screens, global reach.

---

## 7. Investor-Level Branding
- The site structure explicitly caters to stakeholders beyond advertisers: landlords (property owners who want screens on their buildings), governments/municipalities (city planning), and investors.
- A dedicated, robust "Investor Relations" section is mandatory for the "unicorn/public company" feel.

---

## Strategy for Our Implementation
- **Vibe:** Sleek, dark mode or ultra-clean light mode with high contrast. Needs to feel like a luxury tech brand (e.g., Stripe meets JCDecaux).
- **Layout:** Use Framer Motion for smooth, deliberate scroll animations. Big, bold typography.
- **Content:** Replace all SaaS "Self-Serve Dashboard" concepts with "Enterprise Platform," "Global Network," and "Programmatic AdTech."
- **Colors:** Deep midnight blue/black (#0A0A0A) background, pristine white text, and an energetic accent color (e.g., an electric blue or sophisticated gold) to represent the digital screen light.
